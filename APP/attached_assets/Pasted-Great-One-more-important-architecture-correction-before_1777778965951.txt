Great. One more important architecture correction before closing the Replit mock:

Mapazapp must be designed as multi-account and multi-broker from the beginning.

This is not just an account selector. Multi-account affects balance, equity, daily drawdown, max drawdown, prop firm rules, journal, alerts, backtest parameter sets, bridge status and risk.

Real use cases:
- one PropXP account and one The5ers account at the same time;
- two The5ers accounts;
- one demo account and one funded/challenge account;
- one personal account and one challenge account;
- one Phase 1 account and another Phase 2 account;
- multiple MT5 terminals, each connected to a different account.

Please update the mock UI/data model to support this conceptually.

Important:
This is still mock-only.
Do not implement real backend logic.
Do not connect to MT5.
Do not implement real risk calculations.
Do not implement real account persistence.
Do not implement real trading logic.
Do not implement execution.
